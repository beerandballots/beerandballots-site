Beer and Ballots

An iPhone and iPad app for working through your Los Angeles ballot, alone or with friends. Look up the races and measures for your ZIP code, read about the candidates, keep your picks on your device, and, if you like, talk it over in a private group.

Support · Privacy policy

Beer and Ballots is an independent app. It is not an official election resource and is not affiliated with any government agency, candidate or campaign.
